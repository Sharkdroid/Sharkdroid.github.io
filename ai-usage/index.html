About this hub

This page contains all my conversations with Claude across my own machine and my workbench of my work on the Cascade CMS library and tools. I've organized it by platform: claude-ai are conversations had in the Claude web UI and claude-code are transcripts from the Claude Code terminal. I've also broken each down by project. The goal of this page is to be transparent about my AI usage. Early-stage projects were hand-coded. As AI was starting to get integrated into my workspace, I started using Claude Web/Code to brainstorm, design, and implement these more ambitious ideas of mine. These are my unfiltered transcripts of how I use AI in my workflow and what I learned from it.

Why some conversations show redactions: Before publishing, I replaced credentials, internal hostnames, email addresses, my username, locations and the names of other people with labeled markers such as [redacted: credential]. They appear highlighted in yellow inside the transcripts. Nothing else was edited.
